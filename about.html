About Aurelia Grand

A landmark of quiet luxury in the heart of the city, welcoming guests since 1998.

Rooted in Hospitality

Aurelia Grand was founded on a simple belief: true luxury lies in genuine care. What began as a single boutique property has grown into a landmark hotel, while never losing the personal warmth that defined its earliest days.

Today, our team of dedicated hosts, chefs, and artisans work together to ensure every guest feels not just accommodated, but truly welcomed.
